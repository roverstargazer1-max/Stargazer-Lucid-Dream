# 搜索图标候选：最终提示词

生成方式：内置 imagegen；四个候选分别生成，透明背景。共同风格参考：[细长搜索框](../../assets/design/starry-search/search-frame-v1.png)。

## A · 切角放大镜

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: a small standalone SEARCH ICON candidate for a midnight cobalt-blue blog search field, transparent PNG.
Input image 1 is ONLY a style reference, the matching search-field graphic: borrow shallow angular/chamfered cuts and a restrained graphic-print character, not the long banner itself. Do not copy its entire texture into the icon.
Design system shared by all four candidates: cold white #F5F8FF as the principal stroke, one very small muted icy-cyan #83C8E1 accent under 10% of the mark; no filled background, no icon tile. Matte flat graphic linework. Consistent thin-medium optical strokes (about 2 pixels when viewed at 32px), crisp but not hairline, deliberate diagonal cuts, angular flat terminals rather than bubbly rounded caps. Easily recognized as SEARCH at 24-32px. Editorial Graphic Design + Minimal Futurism, rational, restrained, modest technological character.
Composition: exactly ONE icon, compact square footprint, centered with about 18%-22% transparent padding. Prefer 512x512 PNG, actual mark roughly 300-340px across. True transparent alpha outside all icon shapes. No black backdrop, no white backdrop, no colored background or checkerboard.
Avoid: neon, glow, bloom, metal, glass, 3D, shadows, heavy grain, dense halftone, dense HUD/radar ornament, decorative particles, fine microticks, complex layered lines or gradient. No text, numbers, letters, headings, labels or watermark. No search field or poster in the output. It must remain an isolated icon asset.
Candidate A: recognizable magnifying glass whose lens contour is a simple chamfered square / soft octagon (not a circle), clean hollow center. The upper-left and lower-right corners have shallow diagonal bevels echoing the search banner. One simple handle extends from lower-right at 45 degrees, with a cut flat terminal and a tiny icy-cyan end segment. Single contour only, no extra brackets or crosshair.
```
